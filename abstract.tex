In years online shopping has become a part of everyday life. People shop online every day. Online shopping now changes the way people interact with stores and products. ShopSense: Intelligent E-Commerce Platform is a web based system. It mixes an store, with Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML). It makes product discovery and purchasing smoother and more personal. It uses AI to suggest items that match the shopper’s taste. It learns from each purchase to improve recommendations. Online shopping with ShopSense: Intelligent E-Commerce Platform feels like a talk, with a friend. The system offers a complete shopping pipeline, including dynamic product and category management, advanced search, AI‑driven recommendations, shopping cart, secure multi‑payment checkout, and order tracking. ShopSense is organized into three main components: a customer-facing storefront for browsing products, interacting with an AI chatbot, and placing orders; an intelligent backend layer that powers ML-based semantic search, recommendation algorithms, and analytics; and an admin panel for managing catalog data, coupons, orders, and customer interactions. The platform is implemented using Django and REST APIs on the backend, with integrated payment gateways (such as Stripe and bKash) and a relational database for reliable data persistence. ML models are used to generate product embeddings, track user interactions, and rank search results to deliver more relevant and personalized product lists. By reducing friction in product discovery, automating repetitive support queries through a chatbot, and providing richer insights into customer behavior, ShopSense improves user satisfaction and supports store owners in scaling their online business. This project contributes to the evolving field of AI‑enhanced e‑commerce by demonstrating how web technologies and ML techniques can be combined to build an intelligent, data‑driven online shopping platform.
